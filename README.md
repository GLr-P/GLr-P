# Hi, I'm Alexander 👋

I own and run **[Nano Tech Services](https://nanotech.it.com)**, a small PC repair business. Most of my day goes to fixing, upgrading and cleaning up computers. In my spare time I build free, open-source Windows apps, usually to fix a problem I keep running into myself.

All of my apps are **free, ad-free and open source**. They keep your data on your own computer.

## Projects

### 🩺 [Troubleshootr](https://github.com/GLr-P/Troubleshootr)
A portable, all-in-one Windows diagnostic tool for technicians: the first thing to open when a PC has a problem. It runs from a USB stick with nothing to install. It checks hardware, drivers, updates, startup apps and performance, then explains what it found in plain language.

[![Release](https://img.shields.io/github/v/release/GLr-P/Troubleshootr?include_prereleases&label=release)](https://github.com/GLr-P/Troubleshootr/releases) · C# / WPF · [Website](https://glr-p.github.io/Troubleshootr/)

### 🗂️ [Plannr](https://github.com/GLr-P/plannr)
A calm, all-in-one organizer for Windows: notes, calendar, customers, tickets, money and a locked vault in one app. It's built for small businesses and busy people who want everything in one place, and your data stays on your computer.

[![Release](https://img.shields.io/github/v/release/GLr-P/plannr?include_prereleases&label=release)](https://github.com/GLr-P/plannr/releases) · Electron / React / TypeScript · [Website](https://glr-p.github.io/plannr/)

### 🎯 [RainbwStats](https://github.com/GLr-P/RainbwStats)
An ad-free companion app for Rainbow Six Siege: live lobby stats, operator analytics and pick recommendations based on your own roster.

[![Release](https://img.shields.io/github/v/release/GLr-P/RainbwStats?include_prereleases&label=release)](https://github.com/GLr-P/RainbwStats/releases) · Electron / React / TypeScript · [Website](https://glr-p.github.io/RainbwStats/)
